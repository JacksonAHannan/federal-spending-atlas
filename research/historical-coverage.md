# Historical coverage

Verified November 21, 2026 in response to the user's question. Availability dates are not guarantees of uniform completeness, geography accuracy, or comparability.

- **FY2008 onward:** current USAspending search/geography API. Its documented earliest search action date is October 1, 2007. This is the practical starting point for extending the current aggregation-based dashboard. [Official endpoint contract](https://github.com/fedspendingtransparency/usaspending-api/blob/master/usaspending_api/api_contracts/contracts/v2/search/spending_by_geography.md).
- **FY2001 onward:** the same official contract directs users to custom/download/bulk_download APIs for records dating to October 1, 2000. Extending before FY2008 would therefore use transaction-download ingestion rather than this geography endpoint. The starting date alone does not establish complete grant/contract coverage for every agency or comparable geographic coding.
- **Modern earmarks, FY2022 onward:** GAO's current compilation covers FY2022–FY2024; there were no newly designated CPF/CDS funds in FY2025, and GAO says FY2026 coverage will be added later. [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/tracking-funds). This is a program-series boundary, not the earliest congressional earmarking in U.S. history.
- **Historical earmark transparency files, FY2008–FY2010:** TCS describes these as extensive files and says earlier data are less comprehensive. [TCS](https://www.taxpayer.net/budget-appropriations-tax/earmark-data/).
- **CAGW series, 1991 onward:** the organization says it has tracked pork-barrel spending since 1991. Its classification and records are not a continuation of GAO CPF/CDS geography. [CAGW](https://www.cagw.org/earmark-database/).
- **Older federal archives:** NARA catalogs civilian procurement contract records for FY1978–1997 (primarily awards at least $25,000), FAADS assistance files for FY1981 and FY1982–2010, CFFR geographic spending files for FY1982–2007, Federal Outlays System records for FY1968–1980, and military prime-contract files for FY1965–1975. These have different coverage, thresholds, financial measures, and geographic detail. Their existence does not imply a consistent congressional-district panel can be reconstructed directly. [National Archives catalog overview](https://www.archives.gov/research/electronic-records/reference-report/federal-contracts).

Recommendation (inference): extend this dashboard to FY2008 first if requested; evaluate FY2001–2007 separately before treating it as comparable. Use original-year district boundaries for historical representation questions, or document and validate a fixed-boundary reassignment for place-based comparisons. Older district numbers alone cannot be joined across redistricting cycles.

Current implemented coverage remains FY2022–FY2026 obligations and FY2022–FY2024 GAO projects. The user asked about availability, not an expansion, so historical datasets were not automatically appended.
